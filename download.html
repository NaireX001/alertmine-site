Download Alert Mine for Android (APK)

Installing Alert Mine. Alert Mine is currently distributed through this official website while we prepare for wider app distribution. Because the app is installed directly from our website, Android may ask you to approve installing from your browser. This is a standard Android security step for apps installed outside Google Play. Only download Alert Mine from alertmine.site.

How to install

  1. Download the appOpen this page on your Android phone and tap the download button above. If your browser asks you to confirm, check that the download comes from alertmine.site and that the file is named alertmine.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded fileWhen the download finishes, tap the notification, or find alertmine.apk in your phone’s Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browserAndroid may ask you to let your browser install apps. If prompted, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back to the install screen.
  4. Install Alert MineTap Install. Google Play Protect may offer to scan the app first; you can let it. If you see a security warning you don’t recognise, don’t bypass it: contact our support team and we’ll help.
  5. Set up your accountOpen Alert Mine, create your account, connect your first platform and allow notifications. You’re ready to receive important alerts.

Keeping Alert Mine up to date

We continuously improve Alert Mine and add new features. When a new version is available, the app tells you an update is ready; follow the on-screen steps to download and install it. Updates install over your existing app, so your account and settings stay as they are. We recommend keeping Alert Mine up to date for the latest improvements and security fixes.
