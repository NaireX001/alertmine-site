Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

At Alert Mine, we understand that your email accounts and personal information deserve careful protection. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how we protect it when you use the Alert Mine Android app or visit alertmine.site (“Alert Mine”, “we”, “us”). Our goal is to be transparent about how the service works and what happens to your information. By using Alert Mine, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and authentication details. If you register with email and password, your password is securely hashed by our authentication provider, and we don’t have access to it. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account name and email address.

Connected Gmail accounts

If you connect a Gmail inbox, we collect that Gmail address, the platform you choose to monitor (such as Upwork or Fiverr) and any optional label you add. We also receive OAuth access and refresh tokens from Google, which let Alert Mine perform the Gmail operations described in section 2.

Forwarded emails

If you use email forwarding, Alert Mine receives the emails you forward to your private Alert Mine address. We keep only emails from the platforms you have set up and Gmail’s forwarding-confirmation email (which we use to confirm the forwarding for you). Any other forwarded message is discarded without being stored.

Platform notification data

For emails from the platforms you have connected, we process:

  • Email subject
  • Sender
  • A short message preview
  • Date and time received
  • The email’s message identifier

For forwarded emails, we also store the email’s content so you can open it in the app. For connected Gmail inboxes, the full email is fetched from Gmail when you open an alert. A copy of opened alerts is kept on your phone so they open offline.

Optional profile information

You may choose to add your name, phone number and address.

Device information

To deliver notifications and keep your account secure, we collect your push notification token, a random device identifier (used for device limits), device model, Android version and app version.

Payment information

We keep subscription records: your plan, amount, any discount, transaction date and payment reference. Payments are processed by our payment partners, Korapay and Paystack. Your card and bank details are handled by them and are not received or stored by Alert Mine.

Support communications

When you contact support, we keep the messages and details you send us, plus basic app details (such as plan, app version and phone model) needed to resolve your request. If you send a request from the in-app assistant, your chat with the assistant is included with it.

2. Google user data and Gmail access

This section applies only if you choose Connect Gmail. If you use email forwarding instead, Alert Mine has no access to your Gmail account; it only receives the messages you choose to forward.

What the Gmail permission is used for

Alert Mine requests Google’s gmail.modify permission, and uses it only to:

  • Read your Gmail address, to show which inbox is connected.
  • Search for unread emails from the sender addresses of the platforms you chose (for example Upwork’s notification senders) received in the last 30 days.
  • Read those matching emails, to create alerts and show you the full email when you open one.
  • Receive change notifications from Google for your inbox, so new platform emails reach you quickly. These notifications contain no email content.
  • Keep read status in step: when you open or mark an alert as read in Alert Mine, we mark that same email as read in Gmail; when you read an email, or mark it unread again, in Gmail, Alert Mine follows.

How we limit Gmail access

We don’t read, store or process emails other than those from the platforms you configured. We don’t send, delete, move, archive or label your emails; the only change Alert Mine makes in Gmail is marking a platform email as read. Alert Mine staff do not read your email content, except where you explicitly ask us to as part of a support request, where needed to investigate security issues or abuse, or where required by law.

Limited Use disclosure

Alert Mine’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, use it to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, or use it for any purpose other than providing the features you authorised. Your email content is not sent to our support assistant.

3. How we use your information

  • Providing the service: retrieving your platform notifications, showing alerts and sending push notifications.
  • Account management: creating accounts, signing you in and maintaining your profile.
  • Subscriptions: processing payments, managing plans, applying coupons and sending receipts.
  • Teams: supporting team membership, roles and inbox assignments, and sending team invitations.
  • Security: protecting accounts, preventing abuse and enforcing plan limits.
  • Support: answering questions and fixing problems.
  • Service emails: verification, password-reset, receipts and important account messages.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Service providers and sharing

We share information with trusted providers only as far as each needs it to do its job:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions (our main data infrastructure).
  • Google: Gmail API access, Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notification delivery.
  • Expo: push notification delivery. Notifications contain the platform name and the email subject.
  • Korapay and Paystack: payment processing.
  • Google Gemini: powers the in-app support assistant. It receives what you type into the assistant and a short summary of your app setup (plan, connected platforms and their status, notification settings, phone model and app version). Your emails, email addresses and Gmail data are not sent to it. Google may use these messages to improve its services, and they may be reviewed by people, so please don’t type passwords, card details or other sensitive information into the assistant.
  • Resend: sending our emails (verification, receipts, invitations, support replies) and receiving emails forwarded to Alert Mine.
  • Team administrators: if you join a team, the team owner and admins can see your name and email address and choose which team inboxes you can access.

We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, court order or valid legal process. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

5. Data storage and security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted connections: all data between your phone and our services travels over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encrypted Gmail credentials: Gmail access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only by our servers.
  • Access controls: security rules in our database are designed so each account can reach only its own data.
  • Secure sessions on your phone: your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your information safe and take appropriate steps if we become aware of a problem.

6. How long we keep data

We keep information only for as long as we need it to run Alert Mine, keep proper records and meet our legal obligations, and we delete or anonymise it when it’s no longer needed. In general:

  • Alerts are kept while they’re useful to you. Read alerts, including the content of forwarded emails, are cleared automatically after a period of time, and older unread alerts are also cleared over time.
  • Copies on your phone may stay on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Support requests are kept for as long as needed to help you and handle any follow-up, then deleted.
  • Support assistant chats are not stored by Alert Mine, unless you send them to our team with a support request.
  • Payment records are kept for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, including after an account is deleted.

Removing a connected inbox

When you remove an inbox, its alerts are deleted and Alert Mine stops accessing it. For a connected Gmail inbox, we also revoke Alert Mine’s Google access where no other connection of yours uses that Gmail account.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, inboxes, alerts, profile and devices, and revokes Alert Mine’s Gmail access. Payment records are kept as described above. You can also remove Alert Mine’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Your privacy rights

You can:

  • See and update your profile information in the app.
  • Remove connected inboxes.
  • Delete your Alert Mine account.
  • Ask for a copy of your personal information.
  • Ask how your information is collected and used, or object to a particular use.

To make a privacy request, email support@alertmine.site. We will respond within a reasonable time, and within any period required by applicable law.

8. Children’s privacy

Alert Mine is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may suspend or close it.

9. Cookies and local storage

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The Alert Mine app stores some information on your phone for essential features, such as your sign-in session and offline copies of alerts.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology or legal obligations change. We will update the date at the top of this page and may notify you in the app about significant changes.

11. Contact us

Questions, concerns or requests about this policy or your information: Alert Mine Support, support@alertmine.site, alertmine.site.
