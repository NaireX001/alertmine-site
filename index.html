Need more information?

Good questions. Straightforward answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started with Alert Mine.

Does Alert Mine read all my emails?

No. Alert Mine only receives emails from the platforms you choose to connect, such as Upwork or Fiverr notifications. It doesn’t need, read or store your unrelated personal emails, and it doesn’t send or delete email on your behalf.

Which platforms does Alert Mine support?

Alert Mine currently supports notifications from Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, Freelancer, PayPal and Stripe, with more on the way.

Will I still receive alerts when the app is closed?

Yes. Alert Mine is built to deliver ringing notifications even when the app isn’t open. Delivery also depends on your phone’s settings and Android’s background limits, so the app guides you through the few settings that keep alerts reliable.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes two inbox connections (one per platform) on one device. No payment or credit card is required.

How do I pay for a subscription?

Plans are monthly and priced in Nigerian Naira. Upgrade inside the app and pay securely by card or bank transfer through our payment partner, Korapay. There are no long-term contracts. Payments are generally non-refundable; see our Terms of Service.

Why isn’t Alert Mine on the Google Play Store yet?

Alert Mine is currently in Android early access, so we offer it directly through this official website while we work towards wider distribution. The app updates itself from inside the app.

Is Alert Mine available on iPhone?

Not yet. Alert Mine is currently designed for Android devices.
